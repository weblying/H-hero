Questions people ask first

When do I pay?

Not when you send the request. After our team approves your move and sets the final price, your booking page shows a PayPal checkout for the 20% deposit.

How do I find the driving distance?

Look it up in any maps app and enter the miles. Our team checks the route before confirming your price, so a rough number is fine.

How will I know my request went through?

You get a private booking link right after you submit. It shows the status of your request and is where you pay once approved. If email is set up, we send the link to you too. Keep it somewhere safe.

What if you can't take my move?

We'll tell you on your booking page, with a reason. You haven't paid anything at that point, so there's nothing to refund.

Is paying through PayPal safe?

Payments are handled by PayPal. HaulHero never sees your card or bank details.

How do I reach you?

Send your request and we'll reply on your booking page.
